Scanvas privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Scanvas is a document scanner for Android and iPhone that works only on your phone. It never goes online, so it cannot send your scans or anything else about you anywhere.

What Scanvas collects

Nothing. Scanvas has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no cloud. On Android the app does not ask for internet access, so Android does not let it go online. On iPhone the app contains no code that connects to the internet.

What stays on your phone

  • The photos you take and the pages made from them, kept in the app's own private storage.
  • Files you choose to save (on Android, PDFs go to Download/Scanvas and pictures to Pictures/Scanvas; on iPhone, wherever you pick in Files).
  • Your settings, such as the theme and your last save choices.

A PDF password you type is kept in memory only, for as long as that scan is open, and is never stored.

Camera

Scanvas uses the camera only to scan, and only while the camera screen is open.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's own share list opens and you choose where the file goes. Scanvas hands the file only to the app you pick.

Lifetime unlock

Scanvas is free. An optional one-time payment unlocks extra features. Google Play or the Apple App Store handles that payment under their own privacy policies; Scanvas never sees your card or account details. Scanvas only asks the store app on your phone whether the unlock was bought, and keeps that answer on the phone.

Backups

A backup is a single file saved where you choose. It is not locked, so keep it somewhere safe. The phone's automatic cloud backup is turned off for your scans.

Deleting your data

Delete any scan inside the app. Uninstalling Scanvas removes everything in its private storage. Files you saved to your folders stay until you delete them.

Children

Scanvas is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
